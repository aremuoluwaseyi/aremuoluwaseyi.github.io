CASE STUDY 01

Oracle FLEXCUBE 12.2 → 14.0 migration

Context

A core-banking upgrade affects every connected channel. Polaris Bank nominated representatives from departments whose products depended on the core platform; I represented Digital Banking.

My responsibility

I worked across core banking, digital channels and external integrations to validate customer journeys through integration testing and UAT. Coverage included intra- and interbank transfers, bill payments, loans, airtime and data, statements, USSD and third-party services.

How I contributed

  • Translated digital-channel requirements into test scenarios.
  • Compared expected and observed transaction behaviour.
  • Documented anomalies and coordinated resolution across teams.
  • Supported readiness decisions and go-live confidence.

Why it matters

The project demonstrates requirements clarification, data reconciliation, quality assurance and stakeholder communication in a high-risk transformation—not only technical support.

CASE STUDY 02

Digital banking reliability improvement

Problem

Recurring incidents created repeated escalations, longer resolution cycles and avoidable customer friction.

Approach

I combined incident categorization, SQL analysis, Metabase monitoring, root-cause investigation and structured coordination across internal and external teams.

Measured outcome

60%+reduction in repeat incidents 40%improvement in resolution time

Transferable value

This is the same discipline I bring to analytics: define the problem, improve data visibility, investigate patterns, communicate clearly and measure whether the intervention worked.
