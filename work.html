The analytical question

How do accommodation mix, prices and listing patterns differ across Buenos Aires, Cape Town, Bangkok, Istanbul, Austin and Melbourne?

My approach

I standardized city-level data, explored listing composition and built interactive comparisons that let users move between location, price and room-type perspectives.

Key insight

Entire-home listings dominate across the selected markets, but city-level differences make direct comparison more useful than a single global average.

PYTHON · PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS

Customer Churn Prediction

0.850AUC86.1%accuracyLogisticregression

The business question

Which customer characteristics are associated with attrition, and can a transparent classification model identify higher-risk customers?

Method

I prepared the data, evaluated categorical relationships with chi-square tests, trained a logistic regression model, and assessed it using confusion-matrix and ROC evidence.

Responsible interpretation

Accuracy was not treated as sufficient on its own. AUC and class-level performance were used to assess whether the model could support targeted retention decisions.

R · STATISTICAL ANALYSIS

Canadian Labour Market Analysis

112,776recordsANOVAgroup differencesRegressionrelationships

The research question

How do earnings vary with education, age and gender within the 2024 Labour Force Survey?

Method

I cleaned and recoded survey variables, isolated the employed population, used exploratory visualizations, correlation, hypothesis tests, two-way ANOVA and regression to examine wage patterns.

What emerged

The analysis showed an education-linked earnings gradient and a modest positive age–earnings relationship, while also surfacing a gender wage difference that required careful interpretation.
